Who We Are

Neuropsychological Clarity, Delivered with Compassion

At Neuropsychology Specialists of Austin, we help children, adolescents, and adults better understand how their brains influence learning, memory, behavior, and emotional health. Our evaluations are designed to give families and individuals clarity — not just a diagnosis, but a deeper understanding of strengths, challenges, and the most effective next steps.

We know that seeking an evaluation can feel overwhelming. That’s why we take the time to explain each part of the process, listen closely to your concerns, and create a supportive environment where you and your child feel comfortable and understood. Our goal is simple: to provide answers that equip families and individuals, guide treatment, and support long‑term success at home, in school, and in the community.

Our Story

Neuropsychology Specialists of Austin was founded by Dr. Melissa Bunner and Dr. Stephanie Paulos, building on their extensive experience working with individuals and families throughout the Austin community.

In 2007, Dr. Bunner and colleagues founded Austin Neuropsychology, and Dr. Paulos joined the practice in 2010. Drs. Paulos and Bunner established Neuropsychology Specialists of Austin in 2026 to expand their shared commitment to thoughtful, individualized, evidence-based, and specialized neuropsychological care.

Rooted in decades of clinical experience, the practice was created to provide comprehensive evaluations that look beyond diagnosis to help individuals and families thrive. Through their combined expertise, education, and commitment to individualized care, Dr. Paulos and Dr. Bunner continue to build on the practice's mission to help individuals uncover their strengths, make sense of challenges, and develop individualized strategies. Their evaluations focus on understanding the whole person—not simply identifying areas of difficulty—and translating assessment results into meaningful recommendations and practical next steps.

Today, Dr. Bunner and Dr. Paulos bring specialized expertise in neuropsychological assessment, neuroanatomy, brain development, neurological and developmental disorders, ADHD, autism, and brain injury, while collaborating with families, schools, and other professionals to support meaningful progress.

Our Experts

Dr. Melissa Bunner, Ph.D., ABPP-CN

Dr. Melissa Bunner is a board-certified clinical neuropsychologist with more than 25 years of experience working with children, adults, and families. She earned her doctorate in clinical psychology from The University of Texas at Austin and holds board certification in clinical neuropsychology from the American Board of Professional Psychology (ABPP). At Neuropsychology Specialists of Austin, she provides comprehensive evaluations for individuals with known or suspected neurological or developmental disorders with particular interests in ADHD, Dyslexia, Dyscalculia, Dysgraphia, Autism Spectrum Disorders, and Traumatic Brain Injury.

Dr. Bunner’s approach to assessment focuses on understanding each individual’s unique pattern of strengths and weaknesses and helping families make sense of what those findings mean in everyday life. She believes an evaluation should provide more than a diagnosis; it should give families a clearer understanding of functioning and practical recommendations for moving forward. She frequently collaborates with physicians, schools, therapists, and other professionals to help guide treatment and educational planning.

In addition to her clinical practice, Dr. Bunner has presented on neuropsychological assessment, ADHD, and learning disorders. She is involved in professional and charitable organizations at the local, state, and national levels, supervises doctoral-level graduate students in neuropsychology, and participates in clinical research.

When she is not working, Dr. Bunner enjoys reading, traveling, and attending sporting events. She is an enthusiastic fan of both the Texas Longhorns and the Wake Forest Demon Deacons. She also loves spending time with her animals, volunteering at The Settlement Home, and being with her family.

Dr. Stephanie Paulos, Ph.D., ABPP-CN

Dr. Paulos received her Ph.D. in School Psychology from UT Austin and completed both her internship and two-year neuropsychology fellowship at Kennedy Krieger Institute/Johns Hopkins School of Medicine. She is board-certified (ABPP-CN) and active in the International Neuropsychological Society (INS), American Academy of Clinical Neuropsychology (AACN), and local Austin Neuropsychological Society.

She provides a wide range of services, including comprehensive neuropsychological assessments, cognitive rehabilitation following neurological injury, executive function coaching, Cogmed working memory training, and school advocacy support. Confirm Her specialties include brain injury, spina bifida, cerebral palsy, ADHD, dyslexia, dysgraphia, and autism spectrum disorders.
