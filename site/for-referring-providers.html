For Referring Providers

Referral information for physicians, schools, and attorneys.

Two board-certified neuropsychologists (ABPP). Evaluations for children and adults. What to send, where it goes, and when you get the report.

Referral details

What to send

  • The referral question, stated as a question. "Is this ADHD or a language-based learning disorder?" is usable. "Neuropsych eval" is not.
  • Patient name, date of birth, and contact number.
  • Relevant history: imaging and neurology notes, prior testing of any kind, school records and any current 504 or IEP, medication list.
  • Insurance details, and whether the carrier requires a referral or authorization.

Where to send it

Fax to 737-215-8710. Confirm

There is no dedicated referral portal or referral email address. Confirm

Questions before referring: 512-637-5865. There is no separate provider line that bypasses patient intake. Confirm

Turnaround

Three to four weeks from the testing appointment to the written report. Confirm

The patient is seen for a consultation first; testing is scheduled at that visit. Time from referral to consultation depends on the calendar — call for current availability.

Report delivery

The report goes to the patient at the feedback session. With the patient's written consent, it goes to you as well, and to any other professional involved in their treatment.

Consent is obtained from the patient, not from the referrer. Where a referral comes from a school or an attorney, tell the patient what you have asked for.

Speaking to the clinician

A phone consultation with the evaluating clinician is available on request after the report has been delivered. Confirm

Referral questions we take

Adults

  • Differential diagnosis where cognition has changed.
  • Baseline and serial measurement in a known condition — TBI and concussion, stroke, epilepsy, MS, Parkinson's, Alzheimer's and other dementias.
  • Capacity for return to work or independent living after injury.
  • Treatment and rehabilitation planning.

Children and adolescents

  • ADHD, learning disabilities, dyslexia, dyscalculia, dysgraphia.
  • Autism spectrum disorder.
  • Cognitive effects of acquired injury, genetic and chromosomal disorders, epilepsy, prematurity.
  • A student not progressing, where school data is not explaining why.

Not yet documented

The list of referral questions this practice declines is not published here yet. Confirm

Until it is, call 512-637-5865 before referring anything outside the categories above — particularly forensic and independent medical examination work, where scope has to be agreed in advance.
