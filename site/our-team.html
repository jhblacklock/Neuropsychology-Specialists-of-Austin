Board Certification

What the credential requires

Board certification is not required to practice neuropsychology in Texas, and it is not a membership you buy.

ABPP has been the board-certifying body in psychology since 1947 and is the umbrella for 13 specialty boards, one of which is neuropsychology. It is analogous to the medical specialty boards. Certification requires specialty training, a review of credentials, a written examination, evaluation of submitted work samples, and an oral examination.

What it amounts to is peer acknowledgment of competence to practice in the specialty — other neuropsychologists have read this clinician's actual reports and examined them on them.
