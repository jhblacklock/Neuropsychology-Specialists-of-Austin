## Brand

- **Gold `#B8842F`** — accent only: rules, italic type, small emphasis. Never
  a large background fill.
- **Charcoal `#383A44`** — primary text, dark grounds.
- **Paper `#FDFCFA`** — page background, warmer than pure white.
- **Playfair Display Bold** — headlines and the practice name.
- **Playfair Display Italic SemiBold** — "of Austin", pull quotes.
- **Montserrat SemiBold**, all caps, `0.24em` tracking — eyebrows and labels.

Body copy: one quiet, highly legible serif or humanist sans, chosen once and
used consistently. Not Inter, not Roboto, not Arial.

## The mark

The brain mark is attached as SVG in gold, charcoal, and white. It was
redrawn by hand — two clean closed paths, symmetrical hemispheres, tapered
stroke ends, artboard trimmed to the artwork. An earlier auto-trace was
rejected as lumpy. **Use the SVGs as-is. Do not re-vectorize, redraw, or
regenerate the mark from a raster.**

## The lockup

Mark over stacked wordmark, centered:

1. The mark
2. "Neuropsychology / Specialists" — Playfair Display Bold, two lines
3. "of Austin" — Playfair Display Italic SemiBold in gold, flanked by
   hairline gold rules
4. "BOARD CERTIFIED ASSESSMENT" — Montserrat SemiBold caps

One detail that matters and is easy to lose: the hairline rules are offset
`top: 0.157em`, and the flex row holding them sets an explicit `font-size` so
that `em` resolves against the 34pt italic rather than the inherited size.
This centers the rules on the x-height of the `o` in "of" instead of on the em
box. Without it the rules ride visibly high. Keep it.

## Voice

The reader is usually a worried parent, or the adult child of a declining
parent. The job is to make this feel like the most competent option in
Austin, and to make the process knowable.

- Explain, don't reassure. Precision is the reassurance.
- Short, plain sentences. No marketing verbs.
- Say what a thing is, how long it takes, and what it costs.
- Banned words: journey, empower, holistic, cutting-edge, state of the art.
- No invented patient quotes, outcome statistics, or credentials.
